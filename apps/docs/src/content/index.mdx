## Surfaces

One manifest is served over four protocols:

- **OpenAI-compatible** chat completions at `/v1/chat/completions` (sync + SSE)
- **A2A JSON-RPC** at `/a2a`
- **MCP** at `/mcp/{manifest}`, on the official MCP SDK
- **Direct REST/SSE** at `/chat`

Around them sit the management surfaces — audit, approvals, plans, jobs, manifests, eval, usage,
memory, documents and artifacts — covering observability, human approval, canary manifests and
offline eval. The [Route index](/guide/routes/) lists every route.

The harness is [felix-run/felix](https://github.com/felix-run/felix) (CPython; Compose or Helm).
The chat UI and this site are [felix-run/web](https://github.com/felix-run/web).
